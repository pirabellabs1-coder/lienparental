title: "What is Lien Parental?"
category: "Getting started"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Lien Parental is a mobile app designed for separated parents who want to keep a clear, calm and organized framework around their children.

Lien Parental brings together in one place:

- an encrypted vault for your sensitive documents (judgments, letters, invoices, exchanges);
- a private journal to record facts day by day, with dates;
- a calendar to keep track of custody, medical appointments and activities;
- a space for testimonials written by your loved ones;
- an AI parenting coach that helps you put things into words, step back and prepare your exchanges.

A concrete example: you receive a JAF summons (from the juge aux affaires familiales, the French family court judge). You file the letter in the vault, note the key facts of the last three months in the journal, ask loved ones for a structured testimonial, then the AI Coach helps you prepare your notes.

Lien Parental does not replace your lawyer or a mediator. The app remains neutral between the parents and puts the child's best interests first. For any legal question, consult your lawyer.
