---
title: "How do I organize my documents in the vault?"
category: "Vault"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Lien Parental offers folders and tags to help you find your way, even with several hundred documents.

The default organization:

- Judgments and proceedings
- Health
- School
- Finances and child support
- Communications
- Evidence and statements

You can rename these folders, create new ones, or reorganize everything to suit your own logic.

Tags complement the folders. A single document can carry several tags: "Child Lucas", "2026", "Urgent", "To send to lawyer". Search combines tags, dates and the text recognized in your PDFs.

A concrete example: you are looking for your eldest child's medical expenses for 2026. You filter by the tag "Child Lucas" + "Health" + the year 2026. In three seconds, you get the complete list.

Three practical tips:

- Name your files with the date in YYYY-MM-DD format as a prefix. Chronological sorting becomes automatic.
- Scan as you go rather than in batches that are too heavy.
- Add one tag per child if you have several.

For documents to be presented at a hearing, Lien Parental offers a dedicated "JAF" folder (JAF: juge aux affaires familiales, the French family court judge) with a numbered PDF export. Your lawyer finds everything in the order you have chosen.
