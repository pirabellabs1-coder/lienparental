---
title: "How do I add a testimonial?"
category: "Testimonials"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

A testimonial can be requested in three minutes, without your loved one needing to install Lien Parental.

The steps:

1. Open the "Testimonials" tab.
2. Tap "Request a testimonial".
3. Enter the first name, last name and email address of the person who will testify.
4. Choose a template (grandparent, teacher, neighbor, friend, professional) or leave it blank for a free-form testimonial.
5. Send: your loved one receives a secure link by email.

On their side, your loved one clicks the link, answers a few guided questions (context, facts observed, dates if possible), then confirms. Lien Parental collects the elements of the statement (witness's identity, relationship with the parties, legal statement, copy of their ID, on-screen signature) and offers a pre-filled version to print and sign by hand if the court requires it.

A concrete example: you ask your child's teacher to attest that she consistently sees you at parent-teacher meetings. You receive her dated and signed testimonial in less than 48 hours.

Important:

- The witness must testify to facts they have observed themselves.
- False or favor testimonials expose their author to criminal penalties.
- The final admissibility of a statement in proceedings is assessed by the judge. To check how useful a testimonial is for your case, consult your lawyer.
