---
title: "How do I cancel my subscription?"
category: "Subscription"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Cancelling takes two taps, with no justification needed.

The steps:

1. Open Lien Parental and go to "Profile".
2. Tap "My subscription".
3. Choose "Cancel subscription".
4. Confirm.

What happens next:

- You keep Premium access until the end date of the period already paid for.
- No payment will be taken at renewal.
- Your data stays in your vault.
- If you exceed the free limits (2 GB of space), nothing is deleted: you will not be able to add new items until you switch back to Premium or free up space.

A concrete example: you cancel on October 25, 2026, and your paid period ends on November 14, 2026. You keep all of Premium until November 14, then the account automatically switches to free.

No commitment, no penalty, no phone call to make. You can take out a subscription again later if needed.

If you subscribed through an app store (iOS/Android) back when Lien Parental used that channel, you cancel in your store's subscription settings, not in the app.

For any question about a pending payment, write to support@lienparental.fr.
