---
title: "What exactly does the AI Coach do?"
category: "AI Coach"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

The Lien Parental AI Coach is a conversational assistant designed to help you step back, put things into words and get organized.

What it can do:

- Rephrase a tense message before you send it to the other parent.
- Help you prepare for an appointment (lawyer, mediator, teacher) by listing the points to discuss.
- Summarize a long letter or a judgment into clear key points.
- Suggest questions to ask your lawyer.
- Support you after a difficult exchange, with a few ideas for managing your emotions.
- Suggest words to talk about the situation with your child, depending on their age.

A concrete example: you have just received a hurtful text message. You enter the message and ask the Coach: "Help me reply without making things worse, sticking to the facts about Wednesday's arrangements". It suggests two wordings: you choose, adjust and send.

What it does not do:

- It does not give personalized legal advice.
- It does not take sides between the parents.
- It does not replace a psychologist, a mediator or a lawyer.
- It does not read your other data (vault, journal) unless you explicitly share it with it.

For any legal question about your case, consult your lawyer.
