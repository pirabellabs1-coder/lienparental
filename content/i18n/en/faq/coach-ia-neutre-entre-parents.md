---
title: "Is the AI Coach neutral between the two parents?"
category: "AI Coach"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Yes. Neutrality between parents is a design principle of the AI Coach.

In practice:

- It does not label the other parent ("he is toxic", "she is manipulative"). It comes back to the facts.
- It does not help you "win against" the other parent. It helps you express what you need, for your children and for yourself.
- It always places the child's best interests at the center.
- It does not judge your emotions, but it does not fuel escalation either.

A concrete example: you write "My ex is unbearable, he is stopping me from seeing the children". The Coach will neither confirm nor deny it. It will ask you two questions: what exactly happened this week, and what is the need behind your message. Then it will help you phrase a factual request to the other parent, or prepare a meeting with your lawyer.

Why this approach:

- In the vast majority of separations, both parents experience the situation as difficult.
- A child needs both parents, except in serious situations assessed by the courts.
- Taking sides would not help you move forward, and could lock you into one version of the story.

If you believe there is a dangerous situation, talk to a professional (lawyer, pediatrician, social services, 119, the French national child protection helpline).
