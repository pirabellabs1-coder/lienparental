---
title: "What happens if I lose my password?"
category: "Privacy & security"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Don't worry: you can reset it in a few moments, and your data stays intact.

How to do it:

1. On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot your password?".
2. Enter your account's email address.
3. Open the email you receive (remember to check your spam folder) and tap the reset link.
4. Choose a new password: at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter and one number or symbol.

The reset link is personal and expires quickly. If it has expired, simply start the procedure again.

After the change, we recommend opening Profile, then Security, then Active sessions, and signing out any devices you do not recognize.

If you no longer have access to your email address, contact us from the Help center: we will verify your identity before taking any action.
