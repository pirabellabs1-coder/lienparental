---
title: "Can I get a refund?"
category: "Subscription"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Yes, under the conditions provided for by law and by our terms and conditions.

The right of withdrawal:

- If you subscribe to Premium and change your mind within 14 days, you can request a full refund, unless you explicitly waived this right when subscribing in order to access the service immediately.
- To exercise this right, write to support@lienparental.fr with your account email address.

After 14 days:

- Monthly payments already taken are not refunded.
- The annual subscription may be refunded pro rata in certain exceptional situations (payment error, major technical incident). Requests are reviewed case by case.

A concrete example: you subscribe on October 10, 2026, change your mind on October 20, and write to our support team: you are refunded within 10 business days.

How to make a request:

1. Send an email to support@lienparental.fr.
2. Give the email address of your Lien Parental account.
3. State the reason and the date of the purchase concerned.

We reply within 48 business hours. If accepted, the refund is made to the original payment method.

For any related legal question, consult your lawyer.
