---
title: "What is the difference between the free version and Premium?"
category: "Subscription"
updatedAt: "October 28, 2026"
---

Lien Parental can be used for free, with no ads. Premium expands storage space and the AI Coach, and Coparentalité+ adds sharing between the two parents.

The free version includes:

- 2 GB secure vault.
- 5 AI Coach analyses per month.
- Journal and calendar.
- The essential letter templates.
- PDF export.

Premium, at €11.99 per month or €99 per year (3 months free), adds:

- 10 GB vault.
- 50 AI Coach analyses per month.
- All letter and file templates.
- Advanced chronological journal.
- PDF, Word and Canva export.
- Sync across all your devices.
- Support handled before the Free plan.

Coparentalité+, at €19.99 per month or €189 per year (2 months free), includes everything in Premium and adds:

- 100 AI Coach analyses per month.
- Multiple parents: the other linked parent also enjoys the benefits.
- Enhanced tracking of shared expenses.
- Advanced export for the lawyer or mediator.
- Priority support, handled first.

A concrete example: at the very start of an amicable separation, to gather a few documents in one place, the free version is enough. If you enter JAF proceedings (before the juge aux affaires familiales, the French family court judge), Premium is useful for the AI Coach and all the templates. Coparentalité+ is made for two parents who want to share everything in one place.

Need more analyses? Whatever your plan, including Free, you can buy analysis credits (10 for €2.99, 30 for €6.99, 100 for €19.99). They are valid for 12 months and are only used once your monthly allowance is used up.

You can switch from one plan to another at any time, without losing your data, from My subscription.

For any legal question, consult your lawyer.
