---
title: "What is the difference between the free version and Premium?"
category: "Subscription"
updatedAt: "October 29, 2026"
---

Lien Parental can be used for free, with no ads. Premium expands storage space and the AI Coach, and Coparentalité+ adds a shared family space by invitation.

The free version includes:

- 2 GB secure vault.
- 5 AI credits per month.
- Journal and calendar.
- The essential letter templates.
- PDF export.

Premium, at €14.99 per month or €149 per year (a saving of about 17%), adds:

- 10 GB vault.
- 60 AI credits per month.
- All letter and file templates.
- Advanced chronological journal.
- PDF, Word and Canva export.
- Sync across all your devices.
- Support handled before the Free plan.

Coparentalité+, at €24.99 per month or €249 per year (a saving of about 17%), includes everything in Premium and adds:

- 200 AI credits per month.
- 25 GB vault.
- A shared family space by invitation: you and one other parent, up to 5 child profiles. Nothing is shared without your consent, and your credits remain your own.
- Enhanced tracking of shared expenses.
- Advanced export for the lawyer or mediator.
- Priority support, handled first.

A concrete example: at the very start of an amicable separation, to gather a few documents in one place, the free version is enough. If you enter JAF proceedings (before the juge aux affaires familiales, the French family court judge), Premium is useful for the AI Coach and all the templates. Coparentalité+ is made for two parents who want to share some items in one place.

How do AI credits work? A question to the Coach costs 1 credit, a situation analysis 2 credits, a document analysis 3 credits; the cost is shown before each action. Monthly credits renew every period and do not carry over. Need more? Whatever your plan, including Free, you can buy top-ups (10 credits for €2.49, 30 for €5.99, 75 for €11.99, 150 for €19.99). They are valid for 24 months, are used after your monthly credits and remain yours if you change plan or cancel.

You can move to a higher plan at any time (immediate, prorated difference). A lower plan applies at your next renewal. Your data is always kept, from My subscription.

For any legal question, consult your lawyer.
