---
title: "Does the AI Coach give legal advice?"
category: "AI Coach"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

No. And that is a deliberate choice.

The AI Coach can explain, in simple terms, what certain words you come across mean: alternating residence, visitation and overnight rights, child support, social investigation measure, hearing of the child. It can also help you prepare the questions to ask your lawyer.

But it will never:

- Tell you whether your situation justifies legal proceedings.
- Tell you what your chances are of obtaining a particular decision.
- Draft legal submissions, applications or official legal letters for you.
- Advise you on a litigation strategy.

Why? Because every case has its nuances, and because legal advice involves a professional liability that an AI cannot bear. Bad advice = real consequences for your life and your children's lives.

A concrete example: if you ask "Should I request sole residence?", the Coach will not decide. It will help you clarify your facts, your needs and the points to discuss with a legal professional, and will direct you to your lawyer or to a free consultation (Maison de la Justice et du Droit, a public legal information center, or a point-justice, a free legal advice desk).

For specific legal questions, consult your lawyer. To find a lawyer, consider the aide juridictionnelle (French state legal aid) if your income is modest.
