---
title: "Is my data encrypted?"
category: "Privacy & security"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Yes. Your data is encrypted at every step.

In practice:

- All exchanges between your phone and our servers go through an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
- Your documents, photos and testimonials are stored encrypted (AES-256) on servers hosted in the European Union.
- The most sensitive information, such as your children's medical data, benefits from additional encryption at the application level.
- The download links for your documents are personal and expire after a few minutes.
- Backups are encrypted too.

Each account is isolated: another user can never access your information, even if they know a document's address.

Our teams do not have access to the content of your documents, your testimonials or your conversations with the AI Coach. The administration tool does not allow them to be viewed.

Two practical tips:

- Choose a long, unique password.
- Turn on two-factor authentication in Profile, then Security.

For particularly sensitive documents related to legal proceedings, also consider keeping a copy with your lawyer.
