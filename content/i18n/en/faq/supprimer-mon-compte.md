---
title: "How do I permanently delete my account?"
category: "Account"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Take the time to export what you want to keep beforehand.

The steps:

1. Open "Profile".
2. Tap "Security".
3. Choose "Delete my account".
4. Enter your password to confirm.
5. Confirm the statement "I understand that deletion is permanent".

What happens next:

- Your documents, journals, testimonials and conversations with the AI Coach are erased from our servers.
- Your encrypted backups are destroyed within a maximum of 30 days.
- Your email address is kept for 12 months for accounting and fraud prevention purposes, then deleted.
- Your witnesses receive a notification that your account has been closed. Their testimonials, as documents already sent, remain in their personal archives.

A concrete example: proceedings over, children grown up, you want to turn the page. You first export a complete PDF for your paper archives, then delete the account.

Before deleting:

- Remember the full export (see the question "How do I export all my data?").
- Cancel your Premium subscription if you have one, to avoid a leftover payment.

For any legal question about keeping evidence, consult your lawyer.
