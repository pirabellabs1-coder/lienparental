---
title: "Who can see my information?"
category: "Privacy & security"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

You, and only you.

Each Lien Parental account is isolated: your documents, your journal, your testimonials and your conversations with the AI Coach can only be accessed once you are signed in to your account.

What this means:

- The other parent has no access to your space, unless you decide to share a specific document. Sharing is always voluntary, time-limited and revocable.
- Loved ones who write a testimonial only see their own contribution, never the rest of your file.
- Our support teams can see your email address and technical information (sign-in date, device type) to help you. The administration tool does not allow them to view the content of your documents, testimonials or analyses.
- Like any hosting provider, Lien Parental may be required to respond to a judicial request, strictly within the framework provided for by law.

A concrete case: if you lose your phone, the person who finds it cannot open Lien Parental without your credentials. From another device, open Profile, then Security, then Active sessions to sign out the lost phone.

To send a document to your lawyer, use the PDF export: you then send it through the channel of your choice.
