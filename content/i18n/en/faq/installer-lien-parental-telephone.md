---
title: "How do I install Lien Parental on my phone?"
category: "Getting started"
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Lien Parental is a progressive web app (PWA). You install it directly from your browser, without going through an app store.

On iPhone (Safari):

1. Open the Lien Parental address in Safari.
2. Tap the "Share" button (the square with the arrow).
3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
4. Confirm: the icon appears like any other app.

On Android (Chrome):

1. Open the address in Chrome.
2. Tap the "⋮" menu.
3. Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
4. Confirm.

Once installed, Lien Parental runs full screen, can receive notifications and remains available even without a network connection for viewing your documents that have already been synced.

A practical advantage: no "Lien Parental" entry appears in your list of purchased apps, and you choose the name displayed under the icon. Some parents prefer a neutral name for more discretion.

If the install option does not appear, check that you are using a recent browser and that the page has fully loaded.
