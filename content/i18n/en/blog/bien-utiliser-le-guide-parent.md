Opening a savings account for your child, enrolling them in school, applying for a benefit: every procedure requires specific documents. Forget one supporting document, and that's one more trip. The **Parenting guide** was designed to avoid exactly that.

## Practical guides by territory

Each guide answers the same questions: what the procedure is for, who it concerns, which documents to prepare, which steps to follow and where to go. The guide is available first for **French Guiana**; other territories will follow.

Each guide shows its **date of last verification** and its official sources. Local information that is still to be confirmed is flagged as such.

## The document checklist

Before you set off, tick the documents you have gathered. Your progress is saved: you can see at a glance what is missing.

## Step-by-step pathways

Some periods involve a series of procedures: a baby's arrival, back to school, a separation. **Pathways** group them in order, with a link to each guide.

## Add to Lien Parental in one tap

From a guide, you can:

- file a document in the right vault category;
- add an appointment or a reminder to the calendar;
- record an expense in the finance journal.

The form opens pre-filled: you check it before saving.

## Partner offers

After the neutral information, some guides may present offers. They are always labeled **Partner** or **Sponsored offer**, with objective criteria displayed. The guide never ranks these offers.

The guide does not replace legal advice: for your situation, contact the organization concerned.
