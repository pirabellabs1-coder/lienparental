The start of the school year packs a lot of procedures into a short time. When parents live apart, the risk is not only forgetting a task: it is also that each parent thinks the other has taken care of it. Here is a simple method.

## 1. Make the list together, then share it out

Start with a shared list of tasks, then assign each one to a parent, with a date:

1. Enrollment or re-enrollment (school, canteen, after-school care).
2. School and extracurricular insurance.
3. Buying school supplies and clothes.
4. Certificates and documents requested by the school.
5. Signing up for the year's activities.

The Lien Parental **Parenting guide** details these procedures, with the documents to bring and the organizations involved in your territory.

## 2. Inform the school of the situation

When both parents exercise parental authority, each has the right to be informed about the child's schooling. It is useful to give the school both parents' contact details. A letter template is available in **Ready-to-use documents**.

## 3. Plan ahead for costs

School supplies, canteen, activities: note who pays for which expense upfront and keep the receipts. The **finance journal** lets you record each payment with its attachment, so you can settle up calmly.

## 4. Share the calendar

Back-to-school meetings, class photos, school holidays: add these dates to the shared calendar as soon as they are known. Each parent sees the same information at the same time.

## Key takeaways

- A written, shared list is better than verbal agreements.
- Each task has an owner and a date.
- Receipts are kept as you go.

Official benefits and calendars change every year: always check the information with the organization concerned, for example on [education.gouv.fr](https://www.education.gouv.fr/) (the French Ministry of Education website).
