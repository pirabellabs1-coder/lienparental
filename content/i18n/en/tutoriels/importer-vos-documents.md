Gathering your documents in the encrypted vault takes just a few minutes.

1. **Open the Vault tab.** Padlock icon at the bottom.

   ![step 1](/content/tutoriels/importer-vos-documents/step-1.png)

2. **Tap the "+" button.** Three options: take a photo, scan a document, import from your phone or your cloud.

   ![step 2](/content/tutoriels/importer-vos-documents/step-2.png)

3. **Scan a paper letter.** Place the document on a light surface. Lien Parental detects the edges, straightens the page and improves readability. Scan pages one after another for a multi-page document.

   ![step 3](/content/tutoriels/importer-vos-documents/step-3.png)

4. **Name the file with the date as a prefix.** Recommended format: `2026-10-25-courrier-avocat.pdf`. Chronological sorting becomes automatic.

   ![step 4](/content/tutoriels/importer-vos-documents/step-4.png)

5. **Choose a folder and tags.** For example: the "Proceedings" folder + the tags "Child Lucas" and "2026".

   ![step 5](/content/tutoriels/importer-vos-documents/step-5.png)

6. **Confirm.** The file is encrypted on your device, then synced. It appears in your vault and can be viewed offline.

   ![step 6](/content/tutoriels/importer-vos-documents/step-6.png)
