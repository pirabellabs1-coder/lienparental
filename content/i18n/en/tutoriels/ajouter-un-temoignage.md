A loved one can write a testimonial that complies with Article 202 of the Code de procédure civile (the French Code of Civil Procedure), without installing Lien Parental.

1. **Open the Testimonials tab.** Person icon with a check mark.

   ![step 1](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-1.png)

2. **Tap "Request a testimonial".**

   ![step 2](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-2.png)

3. **Enter the witness's contact details.** First name, last name, email. Specify their relationship to you (family, friends, neighbor, teacher, professional).

   ![step 3](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-3.png)

4. **Choose a guided question template** suited to their profile, or leave it free-form. The template guides the witness on the facts to describe without suggesting any answer.

   ![step 4](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-4.png)

5. **Send.** Your loved one receives a secure link by email. They fill it in from their phone or computer, attach their ID, add the handwritten statement and sign.

   ![step 5](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-5.png)

6. **Retrieve the statement.** As soon as it is confirmed, you receive a notification and the signed PDF is added to your vault, in the "Testimonials" folder.

   ![step 6](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-6.png)

7. **Important reminder.** The admissibility of a statement in proceedings is assessed by the judge. For your evidence strategy, consult your lawyer.

   ![step 7](/content/tutoriels/ajouter-un-temoignage/step-7.png)
