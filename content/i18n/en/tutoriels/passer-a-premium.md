duree_min: 2
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Premium expands your storage space, testimonials and access to the AI Coach.

1. **Open Settings,** then "Subscription".

   ![step 1](/content/tutoriels/passer-a-premium/step-1.png)

2. **Compare the plans.** Free or Premium (€11.99/month or €99/year). Check what is useful to you today: extra space, unlimited testimonials, unlimited AI Coach, OCR, enhanced JAF exports.

   ![step 2](/content/tutoriels/passer-a-premium/step-2.png)

3. **Choose the billing period.** Monthly to try it out, annual if you are in a long period of proceedings (a saving of about 30%).

   ![step 3](/content/tutoriels/passer-a-premium/step-3.png)

4. **Enter your payment method.** Bank card or SEPA direct debit. Payment is processed by a PCI DSS-certified provider. No banking data is stored by Lien Parental.

   ![step 4](/content/tutoriels/passer-a-premium/step-4.png)

5. **Confirm.** Activation is immediate. You receive an invoice by email. You can cancel at any time, with no justification needed.

   ![step 5](/content/tutoriels/passer-a-premium/step-5.png)
