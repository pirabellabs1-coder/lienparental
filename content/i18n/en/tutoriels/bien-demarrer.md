You can get to grips with Lien Parental in five minutes. Follow these steps to start on a solid footing.

1. **Create your account.** Enter your first name, last name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, with one uppercase letter and one number or symbol.

   ![step 1](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-1.png)

2. **Verify your email address.** Enter the 6-digit code received by email. Remember to check your spam folder if you don't see it.

   ![step 2](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-2.png)

3. **Set up your profile.** Add your photo if you wish, then check your first and last name.

   ![step 3](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-3.png)

4. **Add your first child.** First name, date of birth and school grade: that's all. You can add others later.

   ![step 4](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-4.png)

5. **Import a first document.** A judgment, a letter, an invoice. File it in a category (School, Health, Court file…) so you can find it easily.

   ![step 5](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-5.png)

6. **Try the AI Coach.** Paste a message you are hesitating to send: the Coach analyzes the tone and suggests a calmer rewording.

   ![step 6](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-6.png)

7. **Strengthen your security.** In Profile, then Security, turn on two-factor authentication.

   ![step 7](/content/tutoriels/bien-demarrer/step-7.png)
