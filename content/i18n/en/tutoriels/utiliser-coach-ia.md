The Lien Parental AI Coach helps you put things into words, step back and prepare your exchanges. Here is how to get the most out of it.

1. **Open the Coach tab.** It is available from the bottom bar, speech bubble icon.

   ![step 1](/content/tutoriels/utiliser-coach-ia/step-1.png)

2. **Choose a goal.** "Rephrase a message", "Prepare for an appointment", "Understand a letter", "Talk to my child", or a free conversation.

   ![step 2](/content/tutoriels/utiliser-coach-ia/step-2.png)

3. **Give factual context.** The more you describe the facts without judgment, the better the answer. Example: "My ex sent me this text last night at 10 p.m. I'm angry. I want to reply to organize Wednesday."

   ![step 3](/content/tutoriels/utiliser-coach-ia/step-3.png)

4. **Read the suggestions.** The Coach suggests two or three wordings. Choose, adjust, personalize.

   ![step 4](/content/tutoriels/utiliser-coach-ia/step-4.png)

5. **Save the reply** in your journal if you want to keep a record of what you sent.

   ![step 5](/content/tutoriels/utiliser-coach-ia/step-5.png)

6. **Important reminder.** The Coach does not give legal advice. For any question about your case, consult your lawyer.

   ![step 6](/content/tutoriels/utiliser-coach-ia/step-6.png)
