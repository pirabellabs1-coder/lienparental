title: "Exporting your court file (JAF)"
duree_min: 3
updatedAt: "October 25, 2026"
---

Lien Parental generates a numbered PDF file, ready to send to your lawyer for proceedings before the JAF (juge aux affaires familiales, the French family court judge).

1. **Open the Vault,** then the "JAF" folder (create it if it does not exist yet).

   ![step 1](/content/tutoriels/exporter-dossier-jaf/step-1.png)

2. **Tap "Export".**

   ![step 2](/content/tutoriels/exporter-dossier-jaf/step-2.png)

3. **Select the documents to include.** Tick the useful documents. Reorder them by drag and drop according to the outline of your file (judgments, letters, evidence, testimonials, child support).

   ![step 3](/content/tutoriels/exporter-dossier-jaf/step-3.png)

4. **Choose the options.**

   - Cover page with your contact details.
   - Automatic table of contents.
   - Numbering at the bottom of the page (Exhibit no. 1, no. 2...).
   - List of exhibits submitted (bordereau de pièces communiquées).

   ![step 4](/content/tutoriels/exporter-dossier-jaf/step-4.png)

5. **Generate the PDF.** Lien Parental compiles everything, encrypts the file and sends you a secure download link by email, valid for 72 hours.

   ![step 5](/content/tutoriels/exporter-dossier-jaf/step-5.png)

6. **Send the PDF to your lawyer** through the channel they prefer (secure email, dedicated platform). For any question about the content of the file, consult your lawyer.

   ![step 6](/content/tutoriels/exporter-dossier-jaf/step-6.png)
